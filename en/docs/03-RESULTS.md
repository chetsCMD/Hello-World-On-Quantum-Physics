## Sensitivity

- **Each extra byte of code** costs 8 bits, i.e. ×256 in waiting time (+2.41 orders of magnitude).
- **Barrier thickness.** Tunneling depends exponentially on d: shrinking the barrier from 5 nm
  to 1 nm gives 2κd = 12.76 instead of 63.78, i.e. T ≈ 10⁻⁵·⁵ instead of 10⁻²⁷·⁷.
- **Coincidence window Δt.** Models B/C: time ~ Δt^(1−n). A long window (bit retention)
  helps enormously, but real DRAM refreshes.
- **K.** The number of positions K reduces the time by a factor of K (log₁₀K ≈ 11 for 16 GiB).

## How to read this

A number of the form 10^X years with X ≫ 10 means "practically never". But "practically never"
is not the same as "never": the probability is strictly positive, so the event is physically allowed.
